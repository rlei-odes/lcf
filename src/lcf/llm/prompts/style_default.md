Write plainly and concretely. Prefer short sentences and ordinary words.

State what is known, with the numbers, dates and names that were supplied. Do not
pad, do not editorialise, and do not use marketing language or superlatives.

Where a value matters, give the value and its unit rather than describing it
qualitatively. Keep observation and interpretation apart: say what was measured or
seen, and mark anything inferred as inferred.
