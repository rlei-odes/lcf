{# --- questions -------------------------------------------------------------- #}

Asked before anything is written

This is how a section gets written without the author having to write it. They answer a few short questions; the assistant drafts the content from those answers and proposes it; they accept, edit or decline. Ask for the facts only they know — the answers themselves never appear in the document, so ask plainly rather than in finished prose.

A section with no questions still works: the author writes it themselves, or the assistant drafts from whatever notes they pasted in.
