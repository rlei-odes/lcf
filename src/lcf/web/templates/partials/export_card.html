The override field is rendered inline when the gate has not passed, rather than after a refused attempt. Downloads have to be ordinary form posts (a file cannot come back through an htmx swap), so a refusal would otherwise navigate the browser to a bare fragment with no page around it. `data-export-card` is the URL static/export.js asks for once a download has finished — the export it just made belongs in the list below. Without it the form still posts and still downloads; the list is then a reload behind. #}
