What happens when you publish

A version is added. Documents already under way keep the version they started on, wording and checks included, so publishing can never change a report someone is halfway through writing.

That is also why a published version cannot be edited: opening one for editing opens the next one. Before publishing, it is worth reading the type back as a whole, and reading what the assistant will be told.
